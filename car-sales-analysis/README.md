# 🚗 Car Sales — Business Data Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes a car sales dataset to identify patterns in customer purchases, vehicle preferences, dealer performance, geographic sales, and sales trends over time.

The analysis was developed in Excel using pivot tables and visualizations to transform raw sales data into business-oriented insights.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective is to understand the characteristics of car sales and identify patterns that could support business decisions.

The analysis explores:

- Popular car manufacturers
- Customer demographics
- Dealer performance
- Geographic sales distribution
- Vehicle body-type preferences
- Engine types
- Car colors
- Sales trends over time
- The relationship between customer income and vehicle price

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains approximately **23,696 car sales records** and includes information about:

- Customer ID
- Gender
- Annual income
- Dealer
- Car company
- Car model
- Vehicle body style
- Engine type
- Transmission
- Color
- Price
- Dealer region
- Sale date

The analysis uses pivot tables and charts to summarize the data and answer business questions.

## 🏷️ Popular Car Companies

The analysis identified **29 different car brands** in the dataset.

Chevrolet, Dodge, and Ford were among the most frequently sold manufacturers.

![Popular Car Companies](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vguimaraeslima-ctrl/projects/car-sales-analysis/car-sales-analysis/screenshots/01-popular-car-companies.png)

This analysis helps identify which manufacturers have the strongest representation among the recorded sales.

## 🏆 Top Performing Dealers

Dealer performance was analyzed using total sales value.

The analysis focused on the **top 10 dealers** to make differences in performance easier to interpret.

![Top Dealers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vguimaraeslima-ctrl/projects/car-sales-analysis/car-sales-analysis/screenshots/02-top-dealers.png)

## 🗺️ Geographic Sales Distribution

Sales were also analyzed by state to identify the regions generating the highest sales values.

![Sales by State](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vguimaraeslima-ctrl/projects/car-sales-analysis/car-sales-analysis/screenshots/03-sales-by-state.png)

Texas and Wisconsin were among the strongest-performing states in terms of total sales value in the analyzed data.

The geographic view makes regional differences easier to identify than a simple table.

## 📅 Sales Trends Over Time

Total sales value was analyzed across 2022 and 2023 to identify changes over time.

![Sales Over Time](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vguimaraeslima-ctrl/projects/car-sales-analysis/car-sales-analysis/screenshots/04-sales-over-time.png)

The analysis shows fluctuations in monthly sales value and indicates stronger overall sales performance in 2023 compared with 2022.

## 🚙 Vehicle Body-Type Preferences

The dataset was also analyzed to identify the most frequently sold vehicle body types.

![Body Type Sales](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vguimaraeslima-ctrl/projects/car-sales-analysis/car-sales-analysis/screenshots/05-body-type-sales.png)

The most common body types were:

| Body Type | Share of Sales |
|---|---:|
| SUV | 27% |
| Hatchback | 26% |
| Sedan | 18% |
| Passenger | 17% |
| Hardtop | 12% |

SUVs and hatchbacks represented the largest shares of recorded vehicle sales.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several patterns:

- The dataset contains **29 different car manufacturers**.
- Chevrolet, Dodge, and Ford were among the most frequently sold brands.
- A small group of dealers generated substantially higher sales values than others.
- Sales performance varied significantly across geographic regions.
- Overall sales value was stronger in **2023 compared with 2022**.
- SUVs and hatchbacks were the most common body types in the dataset.
